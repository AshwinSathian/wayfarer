---
title: Wayfarer 2.5.0 holds itself to its own claims
date: 2026-10-15
description: Accessibility and performance budgets in CI, a threat model tied to tested claims, and diagnostics that keep your requests out of a bug report.
---

2.5.0 is the release where Wayfarer stops adding features for a while and
checks the ones it has. Most of what changed is in CI, not in the app.

## What CI now holds the app to

Every view is scanned with axe against WCAG 2.2 A and AA, at every impact
level, and the scan finds nothing. The app can be used with a keyboard
alone.

Performance has budgets too: Lighthouse on the desktop preset at 90 or
more, the largest paint within 2 seconds, and blocking time within 200 ms.
Typing in the editor has to answer within 100 ms a key with five tabs
open. CI also checks that the code editor, the script engine, GraphQL, the
OpenAPI importer and the YAML parser stay out of the first load.

## A threat model with a test behind each answer

[The threat model](../../../../docs/threat-model.md) goes through each trust boundary,
lists its threats by STRIDE, and names the tested claim behind each
mitigation. If the claim's test goes away, the build fails.

## Diagnostics that do not leak

Settings, Diagnostics, shows the version and commit, the browser, storage,
the database, the service worker and the Local Bridge, runs self-tests,
and keeps the last 200 errors and Content-Security-Policy violations.
Copying the report, or opening a GitHub issue with it, carries nothing of
a request you sent.

## Before you update

Files now have one shape, with no number in their `$id`. A file an earlier
version exported is refused, so export again after you update. Local
Bridge 3.0.0 relays at `/relay` and keeps its token in
`~/.wayfarer-bridge`: update it and pair again.

The whole list is in [the changelog](../../../../CHANGELOG.md).
