# Reach plan

Where Wayfarer is listed and announced, what goes there, and when (W3.2 of
[the design plan](../../PLAN-design.md)). **Nothing here is posted until the
maintainer approves that entry**, and each entry's status says whether it
has been. The rules quoted were read on 2026-10-19.

The first date for all of it is the deploy of v2.6.0, the release that puts
the website at `/` and the app at `/app/`: before it, a link would open the
old app with no site around it.

## Channels

### Show HN (Hacker News)

- **The rule:** Show HN is "for something you've made that other people can
  play with", easy to try "ideally without signups or emails"; not a landing
  page, and not a version bump
  ([Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html)).
- **Why it fits:** the app opens in a tab, with no account and nothing to
  install.
- **What is posted:** a link to the site's front page, titled
  "Show HN: Wayfarer, an API client that runs in a browser tab, with no
  account", and a first comment by the maintainer: what it is, the claims
  ledger, and where it is behind ([the comparison index](https://wayfarer.ashwinsathian.com/compare/#behind)).
- **When:** after the v2.6.0 deploy, once.
- **Status:** not posted.

### AlternativeTo

- **What is there:** the Postman page lists 128 alternatives, Bruno,
  Insomnia, Hoppscotch and Yaak among the first four
  ([AlternativeTo: Postman alternatives](https://alternativeto.net/software/postman/)).
  The page offers "Add Alternatives".
- **What is posted:** a listing for Wayfarer as an alternative to Postman,
  Insomnia and Bruno: the hero line, "Open source (MIT), web", the site's
  address, and the app's picture.
- **When:** after the v2.6.0 deploy.
- **Status:** not posted.

### OpenAlternative

- **What is there:** a directory of open-source alternatives, with a submit
  page ([openalternative.co/submit](https://openalternative.co/submit)); what
  it asks for is behind a sign-in, so it is read again on the day.
- **What is posted:** the repository and the site, as an alternative to
  Postman.
- **When:** after the v2.6.0 deploy.
- **Status:** not posted.

### The comparison pages, as pages people search for

- **What is there:** `/compare/` and a page for each of Postman, Bruno,
  Yaak, Hoppscotch and Insomnia, plus "Moving from Postman" (W2.3). Their
  titles and descriptions are W3.1's.
- **What is done:** nothing to post; the pages are linked from the front
  page's footer and from each other.
- **Status:** live with the v2.6.0 deploy.

### The blog

- **What is there:** `/blog/` and its RSS feed (W2.5).
- **What is posted:** each post on the site first. A post may be published
  elsewhere only with a link back to its address on the site.
- **Status:** two posts, live with the v2.6.0 deploy.

### The repository

- **What is there:** the description and the website field name the app.
  Its topics are `angular`, `indexeddb`, `api-client`, `api-testing`,
  `developer-tools`, `http-client`, `local-first`, `primeng`, `rest-client`
  and `typescript` (read 2026-10-19).
- **What is proposed:** add `postman-alternative` and `offline-first`, and
  take out `primeng`, which the app no longer uses.
- **Status:** not changed; the maintainer changes them.

## Not on the list, and why

- Paid listings and ads: the site measures no visits (W3.6), so a paid
  listing could not be judged.
- Forums whose rules limit posts about one's own project: each one's rules
  are read before anything is proposed for it, and it is added here first.
