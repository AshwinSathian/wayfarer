How a request leaves when neither it nor its collection says. Auto sends from the page, and through the bridge when the browser could not: a request the server cannot have received is sent again at once, and one it may have received is sent again only if you say so. Direct always sends from the page. Local Bridge sends every request from the bridge on this machine, where CORS and mixed content do not apply.
