[2026-10-23 review's section 6](../hig-review-2026-10-23.md#6-unverified)

Firefox 157 and WebKit 27.2 on macOS. Firefox's and WebKit's are kept here,
WebKit's at 1× (it draws at 2×); Chromium's are the design capture's.

What the table rests on:

- **Nothing is wider than the window** in any state and engine: the
  page's `scrollWidth` was at most the window's in all 36 captures.
- **The error states say the same thing in three engines.** The status
  line says "No response" and the body the same paragraph for a refused
  port, CORS and a name that does not resolve; offline has its own. The
  one line that differs is the record's "What was reported", which quotes
  the engine: "Failed to fetch" (Chromium), "NetworkError when attempting
  to fetch resource." (Firefox), "Load failed" (WebKit). That is the
  browser's evidence, quoted as such (W-7, U-051).
- **Forced colours in WebKit** is a media query Playwright emulates
  (`matchMedia` matched in all three), but WebKit draws no system colours:
  Safari has no forced-colours mode. Chromium and Firefox draw them; the
  buttons keep their borders and Send gains one, as `button.css` says.
- **A larger default text size** is the setting a reader changes; a root
  font size of 200% is what text-only zoom does, and a media query reads
  only the first. With breakpoints in px the first kept the wide layout
  (U-153, fixed in #542); with them in em, a 1,440 px window at 32 px text
  is laid out as a 720 px one. The second still narrows the composer
  beside the tree, which Params did not survive (U-155, fixed in #543);
  the other panes did.
- **RTL** mirrors the same in three engines. Units still read before their
  number ("ms 14.0", "B 60"), the review's W-41g, which waits for a plan
  that takes up a right-to-left language (none does).

## 2.5.8 targets, without ancestors

Every control of D0.1's 49 screens at 1,440 and 390 px in Chromium (a
size is layout, the same in each engine): its own box, not a parent's;
one inside another counted once, as the outer. A target under 24 px on a
side passes when a 24 px circle on its centre meets no other target and no
other small target's circle (WCAG 2.2's spacing exception); a link inside
a sentence is excepted.

- **1,440 px: no failure** on any of the 49 screens. None of the review's
  four suspects (the 8 px splitter, the 16 px checkbox, the 18 px response
  textbox, a 20 px vault button) is among the failures at either width.
- **390 px: one control fails, on three screens:** the Params row's
  checkbox ("Send this parameter" on `first-run` and `method-menu`, "Send
  kind" on `composer-params`). Stacked under the Params section's header,
  its centre was 11 px from the header's edge, 1 px short. U-154. The
  stacked layout it sat in is gone with U-155 (#543), which puts the box
  beside the name; the next pass measures it again.
